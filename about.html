CHAPTER 01

THE TENSION OF SPORT & DRAPING

Inspired by the iconic monochrome geometry of Adidas archival tracksuits, Adabel dissects conventional streetwear patterns. We replace loose cuts with articulated kinetic joints, engineered bar-tack reinforcements, and tactile three-stripe seams.

Each garment undergoes rigorous tensile stress tests before release. We build outerwear not just to look striking in high-contrast editorial photography, but to perform under harsh real-world conditions.

CHAPTER 02

SUSTAINABLE PERFORMANCE MATERIALS

Our technical shells are constructed using 100% ocean-reclaimed polyamide and PFC-free water-repellent coatings. Every button and magnetic clasp is manufactured from recycled aeronautic-grade aluminum alloy.

We operate in limited capsule runs to eliminate inventory waste and preserve the rarity of every Adabel piece owned by our collectors.

CHRONOLOGY

THE ARCHIVE PROGRESSION

2023 // THE FIRST EXPERIMENT

Underground Atelier Launch

Adabel begins as a closed-door bespoke design unit custom-crafting performance tour jackets for electronic music pioneers and underground football collectives.

2024 // MONOCHROME MANIFESTO

The First Public Capsule Drop

Release of the 520 GSM Monolith collection in Tokyo and London. 300 pieces sold out within 14 minutes, cementing Adabel's signature triple-stripe silhouette.

2025 // HYBRID RUNNER SNEAKER DEBUT

Footwear Division Engineering

Partnering with specialized Portuguese cobblers, the brand delivers its inaugural kinetic runner series incorporating PrimeKnit weaving and responsive foam dampers.

2026 // THE PROTO-7 ERA

Global Flagship & Direct Ateliers

Unveiling the full modern online flagship store. Delivering worldwide express bespoke fashion drops with solar neon accents and sustainable modular tailoring.
